# RAW denoising research sweep — 4 November 2026

**Recommendation:** spend the next capture session on independent, native-resolution burst references and a temperature/exposure-matched dark library. Test dark-shading subtraction and sampled dark residuals before training a learned noise generator. Keep `r4_plain` as the frozen comparator. There is no verified paper result that establishes a replacement for it on our cameras.

This is a research plan, not an experiment report. Web search was available and used for primary papers, author pages and runtime documentation, concentrating on 2023–2026 with older methodological anchors. Verification is described per reference below; some full-text fetches failed even where indexed primary-paper text was available. Search is not exhaustive, and preprint claims are not independently reproduced. All costs and proposed thresholds below are engineering estimates, not measured outcomes.

## Local evidence and constraints

Read: `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, docs 01/02/03/05/06/09, `docs/references.md`, the main checkout's `runs/round6/RESULTS.md` and `runs/round7/RESULTS.md`, and the relevant training/evaluation scripts. Earlier architecture/approach proposals are historical; current deployment and results take precedence.

- **Baseline:** NAFNet w40, four stages, encoders `[1,1,2,4]`, middle 6, decoders `[1,1,1,1]`, 23.8M parameters. Packed order is **R/G1/B/G2**, not row-major RGGB. Inputs retain negative black-subtracted values. The model has a sign-preserving gamma head and direct output. Deployment uses 512-packed tiles and mirrored aprons; measured baseline latency is 38.9 ms/tile on the owner's ANE configuration. Parameter count did not predict speed: the 5.9M three-stage alternative took 39.5 ms.
- **Useful change so far:** same-colour binning reduced target noise. Targets remain imperfect and spatially smoother than native images; binning also changes colour-site geometry. The current loss is Charbonnier, epsilon `1e-5`, plus `0.25 ×` gamma-domain loss. Synthetic corruption already includes broad shot/read noise, structured components, impulses and colour-gain augmentation.
- **Round 5:** low-ISO specialisation, more training, more images and stronger gamma loss changed dev PSNR by at most about 0.05 dB. Noise-map conditioning has not established a replacement; another metadata plane alone is not a new data source.
- **Round 6:** calibrated noise used dark fits but **interim, unmeasured shot gain K**. Own-model synthetic gains up to 5 dB on one slice did not transfer to real bursts. Both calibrated and wider priors gained about 1 dB at A7 III ISO 204800; widening lost 0.48 dB overall at X1D II ISO 3200, including 1.32 dB on its centre frame. This supports a narrowly scoped extreme-noise fix, not global widening or a claim that all calibration is ineffective.
- **Round 7:** neighbour-pair self-supervision used 6,195 archive crops but only 54 X1D II crops. X1D II real-burst scores fell by up to 0.12 dB; synthetic slices and residual-noise diagnostics worsened. Same-day siblings may contaminate some no-reference comparisons. Increasing that objective's weight or collecting more arbitrary noisy crops is low priority.

Every proposed training lineage starts from random initialisation; no external checkpoint, denoised image, pseudo-label, learned perceptual feature or external output used for selection is eligible. The experiments below can be performed entirely with owner captures and independently implemented mathematics. No weights or datasets were downloaded for this sweep. Published benchmark figures are literature evidence, not candidate-selection signals. Own-model distillation, if attempted, must be explicitly recorded as distillation and cannot turn teacher outputs into independent clean references.

Commercial eligibility is separate from scientific usefulness. The [SIDD author page](https://abdokamel.github.io/sidd/) explicitly grants MIT terms for dataset and code; exact files, target construction and notices still need a manifest audit. The [AIM challenge page](https://sonyresearch.github.io/AIM2025_Denoise_Challenge/) explicitly excludes commercial use. Other dataset/code licence statements in `docs/references.md` were **not re-audited here**: do not promote them to current permissions. LRID/LED restrictions, undocumented SID/ELD/LRD terms and unresolved share-alike obligations make owner data the default. Public papers are design references, not blanket permission to copy implementations or assets.

## What the literature does and does not justify

**Noise synthesis.** ELD [R1] remains a useful physical decomposition, but its scalar parameters do not describe every readout pattern. PMN [R2] separates dark shading from random corruption; it also uses real pairs and shot-noise augmentation, so it is not evidence for simply fitting a heavier read-noise tail. SFRN [R3] samples actual sensor noise. Li et al. 2025 [R4] combine shot-noise synthesis with sampled, dark-shading-corrected dark frames and report robustness to approximate K. That supports a cheap empirical baseline, not copying an ISO-to-K formula across sensors. ([ELD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.12751), [PMN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.06103), [SFRN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.04756), [one-hour synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00045))

LRD [R5] combines physical shot noise with learned signal-independent noise. PNNP's revised 2026 paper [R6] learns a physics-informed proxy from darks. Noise Flow [R7] supplies a conditional-density approach; its original signal-conditioned model is not identifiable from darks alone. Our usable variant would learn only the dark/read residual and retain a measured shot term. Dark Noise Diffusion [R8] adds positional and noise-specific modelling; its generation cost is a poor first trade unless simpler samplers demonstrably miss real residual structure. None proves that a generator trained on our small dark library will improve our denoiser. ([LRD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16508), [PNNP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.09126), [Noise Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.08453), [Dark Noise Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11262))

**Challenge evidence needs qualification.** The AIM 2025 report [R9] compares synthesis and architectures, including convolutional and transformer approaches. Its VMCL ablation favours direct RAW processing over its VST variants; it also describes cleaning training images with a separate blind denoiser. That target-cleaning step is ineligible here. The report does not establish that every winning recipe is usable, or that NAFNet plus darks universally wins. ([Report, especially §5.3 and Table 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06601v1))

**Cross-camera generalisation.** LED [R10] reports few-shot transfer with two real pairs **per digital-gain setting**, six pairs in its stated example; this is not two arbitrary photographs per camera. YOND [R11] couples noise estimation to an expectation-matched VST and an SNR-guided denoiser. Generalised Anscombe inversion [R12] explains why a naive square-and-subtract inverse is biased near black. A 2026 nonlocal RAW preprint [R13] reports sensor transfer using synthetic noise and noise-level maps, but requires matching/filtering operations whose ANE cost is unknown. These are hypotheses for controlled tests, not evidence that camera identity disappears after normalisation. ([LED](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/papers/Jin_Lighting_Every_Darkness_in_Two_Pairs_A_Calibration-Free_Pipeline_for_ICCV_2023_paper.pdf), [YOND](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03645), [GAT inverse](https://webpages.tuni.fi/foi/papers/OptGenAnscombeInverse-doublecolumn-preprint.pdf), [nonlocal RAW](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17453))

**Real references.** SIDD [R14] motivates repeated, static measurements and careful target estimation. Burst alignment/fusion work [R15] motivates noise-aware registration and motion rejection; copying a finished fusion output would violate provenance. Our reference must be built from our measurements with documented processing. RawNIND [R16] is useful evidence for sensor-diverse real data, but is not an automatically cleared training pool. Same-exposure repeats are the least ambiguous initial target; base-ISO/long-exposure pairs additionally need per-channel photometric calibration and lighting checks. ([SIDD](https://abdokamel.github.io/sidd/), [burst paper](https://hdrplusdata.org/), [RawNIND](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08924))

**Self-supervision: distinguish methods and baselines.** Noise2NoiseFlow [R17] uses **independent noisy captures of the same signal**, jointly fitting a density model and denoiser; it reports improvements over supervised and weakly supervised baselines. Blind2Sound [R18], a 2025 single-image method, reports SIDD RAW benchmark PSNR 50.92 versus 50.61 for its N2C baseline. This is evidence that self-supervision can beat particular supervised baselines on real noise; it does **not** establish superiority to a strong, camera-matched, supervised synthetic NAFNet. Architectures, targets and protocols differ. No verified comparison here answers that stronger question for our cameras. Also, papers sometimes call dark-based synthetic training “self-supervised”; that is not the archive neighbour-pair objective tested in round 7. ([Noise2NoiseFlow](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Maleky_Noise2NoiseFlow_Realistic_Camera_Noise_Modeling_Without_Clean_Images_CVPR_2022_paper.html), [Blind2Sound, Table 4](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Liu_Blind2Sound_Self-Supervised_Image_Denoising_without_Residual_Noise_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf))

Likely round-7 failure mechanisms are **inferences**, not measured diagnoses: adjacent positions have different underlying texture; subsampling changes apparent spatial scale; residual row/column or fixed-pattern correlation violates independence; the self-consistency prediction cannot supply missing signal; and cross-camera sampling overwhelmed the scarce target-body crops. These follow from `selfsup.py`'s half-resolution adjacent-pair construction and the run's data counts. Test each with true temporal repeats, covariance measurements and matched camera sampling before blaming optimisation. The code's independence assumption should be measured per shutter/ISO/exposure, especially in shadows.

**Efficient architectures.** NAFNet [R19] is already a strong deployment baseline. Restormer [R20], MambaIR [R21] and MambaIRv2 [R22] provide long-range modelling, but their published restoration gains are not ANE latency or native Bayer-denoising measurements. “Restormer-lite” and “MambaIR-lite” below mean proposed reduced local configurations, not verified named mobile RAW models. Apple's deployment guidance [R23–24] makes graph layout, precision and device execution relevant; it does not certify these backbones. Re-parameterisation can fold compatible linear branches; it cannot fold away NAF gates, attention or selective scans. ([NAFNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.04676), [Restormer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.09881), [MambaIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15648), [MambaIRv2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15269), [ANE guidance](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/neural-engine-transformers))

**Texture and clipping.** There is no loss that guarantees truthful texture in an underdetermined single frame. Test small, deterministic, target-referenced gradient/multiscale terms on independent native references. Learned perceptual/GAN/diffusion restoration losses are poor fits to the provenance and fidelity requirements. Raising gamma-loss weight already failed. Clipping also needs a separate contract: estimate the observable, noise-free **clipped** RAW signal and preserve saturated measurements conservatively; do not ask a denoiser to invent scene radiance above sensor saturation. These are proposed design choices, not performance claims from a particular paper.

## Shared experiment and evaluation protocol

All ten experiments use this protocol, with additions specified per experiment. Keep every original measurement read-only. Future caches, dark maps, metrics and checkpoints belong in a dedicated worktree run directory, never the frozen checkpoint directory. This document does not implement the experiments.

**Training and costs.** Use random initialisation for each new base denoiser, noise proxy and student. A matched scratch r4-recipe replay is the experimental control; the historical `r4_plain` is an additional frozen comparator. Short **24k-step scratch pilots only compare against a matched 24k scratch control**; they cannot establish superiority to the 120k baseline. Confirm promising arms at 120k steps, two seeds, before spending acceptance data. Few-shot adaptation starts from our own scratch-trained generic base, preserving that provenance chain; no third-party initialisation is involved.

Budget assumption: 128-packed crops (the r4 recipe), effective batch 16, mixed precision where supported, **0.5–2 optimiser steps/s on one unspecified 16 GB GPU**. This is deliberately broad and unbenchmarked. Measure 500 timed steps after warm-up first; reduce microbatch and accumulate to batch 16 if needed. One 24k arm costs **3.3–13.3 GPU-hours (G h)**; one 120k arm costs **16.7–66.7 G h**. Tables round these to **4–14 / 17–67 G h**. Two independent arms on two GPUs take approximately the per-arm time in wall hours, with twice the GPU-hours. The trainer currently selects one device; do not assume DDP or combined 32 GB memory. Larger crops/attention/scan models need fresh throughput and memory measurements. Capture, preprocessing, export and engineering time are additional. A shared full scratch control can be reused only when data, steps, seed and schedule actually match.

**Frozen real-burst comparison (E1).** Run `scripts/eval_burst_loo.py` with identical group spec, masks, crop coordinates, pre-pass and alpha=1 for all arms. Preserve all round-6 groups separately: A7 III ISO 1250/6400/25600/204800; X1D II ISO 3200 and both ISO 25600 groups. Report per-frame, per-group, flat and texture errors; do not hide the ISO-3200 regression behind an average. Add at least ten new held-out scenes per claimed camera, split by capture session/day before training. Bootstrap paired deltas by **scene/burst**, not correlated pixels or repeated LOO frames. Use two training seeds and report intervals and worst groups.

The current script subtracts estimated target-mean variance from MSE and uses a **99.5th-percentile scene peak**, not fixed unit peak. Preserve that convention for historical comparisons and also label any separately added fixed-range PSNR. Corrected MSE can be non-positive; report it as unresolved at the reference noise floor, not infinite quality. Shared fixed-pattern bias remains in all frames and can reward reproducing the bias. Gain estimates/masks use burst statistics, so the ideal independent-noise correction is approximate. The script assumes pre-aligned bursts and excludes locations where any mean channel exceeds **0.9**. It does not supply independent GT, registration, highlight scoring or all metrics required by docs/05. Frozen historical bursts are now development evidence, not untouched acceptance.

**Dev slices (E2).** Run `scripts/evaluate_dev_v2.py` unchanged on X1D II and A7 III: three deterministic 256-packed crops/source, exposure 1/1, 1/16, 1/64 × low/medium/high, tails, extreme and binned-reference slices. Report channel bias, colour error, flat/texture PSNR and source-paired deltas. Conditioned candidates additionally need oracle/half/double/estimated-map comparisons. Do not train or tune on validation source hashes/days. A camera-calibrated synthetic benchmark can diagnose the simulator but cannot select a release by itself. Other bodies need their own real captures and equivalent development manifests; successful inference is not transfer validation.

**Native references and visuals (E3).** With new independent targets, add packed RAW and fixed-render PSNR/SSIM, per-channel bias, edge/texture crops and highlight masks. These additions require separate evaluation work; LOO does not already produce them. Inspect at alpha=1 with identical black/white levels, white balance and rendering exposure, including shadows, bark/fabric/hair/text, stars, impulses and highlight transitions. Owner blind review must include at least 20 random crops plus named stress cases, including the evaluation-only X2D II ISO 12800 case. Freeze a final acceptance set and use it only after development selection, following docs/05.

**Real RAW without GT (E4).** Existing `scripts/benchmark_cameras.py` proxies and `scripts/audit_real_raw.py` can flag remaining chroma/banding, input/residual correlations, block-mean shifts, impulses and excessive detail removal. Add row/column power spectra and residual-versus-input edge correlation when testing structured noise. A clean-looking residual is insufficient: residuals contain legitimate noise *and* possibly removed scene detail. Noise-left, sharpness, NIQE-like scores and whiteness are not fidelity rankings; no scalar no-reference metric establishes acceptance. Independent repeats permit risk estimates under zero-mean, independent-noise assumptions, but shared bias and registration error break them. SURE-style estimates additionally require a valid noise model and can become another round-6 self-confirmation. Do not import pretrained LPIPS/ARNIQA/TOPIQ or other external learned selection networks. Use matched owner blind review alongside these diagnostics, and mark quality without GT as **not demonstrated**.

**Decision rule.** As a proposed development screening rule, seek at least +0.10 dB scene-median real-burst improvement with a scene-level interval above zero, no repeatable camera/ISO loss worse than 0.05 dB, and no recurring texture/colour artifact. These are triage thresholds, not the owner's acceptance bar. Tiny current-burst differences without enough independent scenes remain inconclusive. Specialist extreme-ISO models must have an explicit scope. Confirm post-export fp32/fp16 parity, flat identity, mirrored borders, tile-phase sensitivity, peak memory and full-frame latency as well as tile latency. Only both quantitative and blind visual acceptance can promote a model.

## Ranked experiments

### 1. Replace binned proxies with independent native burst targets

**Hypothesis / why new:** r4 benefited from removing target noise; its remaining texture ceiling may come from residual target noise and binning geometry. Real native targets change the supervision that rounds 5–7 mostly held fixed. SIDD-style capture is the strongest next data lever [R14], not a guaranteed gain.

**Exact data:** pilot on X1D II: 12 new static scenes, eight training/four development, covering fine print, fabric, foliage indoors, stone, skin-tone chart and deep shadows. At ISO 100/800/3200/25600 capture **36 same-exposure repeats per setting**: four potential noisy inputs plus 32 disjoint target frames. This is 1,728 RAWs. Capture ten *additional* acceptance scenes before selection; retain session/day separation. Pilot ≈181 GB at 105 MB/RAW, before caches. For later cross-ISO long-exposure references capture base-ISO 32–64-frame bursts plus four noisy frames/setting and fit photometric gains; do not treat metadata exposure ratios as exact calibration.

**Procedure / cost:** verify registration from a green preview, then crop/reject or apply a documented CFA-aware warp to each colour plane with its own sampling offsets. Never warp the mosaic as grayscale or use a pretrained denoiser/flow network. Reject motion and inspect edge-difference maps; avoid blurring noisy inputs. `make_burst_targets.py` only averages pre-aligned, matching-metadata frames and catches gross mismatches. It cannot perform cross-ISO matching. Check independent half-burst target differences, target variance, flicker and hot pixels; outlier rejection can bias low counts, so compare with plain means on static valid pixels. Train 50% real pairs/50% existing eligible synthetic replay versus synthetic-only. Two 24k arms: **8–28 G h total**, about 4–14 wall h on two GPUs. Full two-seed candidate plus two-seed matched control: **68–268 G h**, about 34–134 wall h.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** owner measurements only; targets contain no neural output. Do not reuse any historical evaluation scene or its siblings for training. E1/E2/E3, especially native fine-detail and low-ISO shadow slices. Stop if half-burst references disagree at edges or colour-site geometry is uncertain; do not optimise against a blurred target. Four dev scenes are a feasibility pilot, not acceptance coverage.

### 2. Correct stable dark shading before neural inference

**Hypothesis / why new:** a stable spatial offset is not another random-noise scale. Correcting it explicitly may remove banding/colour bias that LOO misses; scalar dark fits and broadening in round 6 did not test a calibrated full-resolution correction [R2, R4].

**Exact data:** per body and shutter mode, use eight ISO settings spanning base to maximum, 64 capped frames at the fastest practical exposure plus 16 at 1 s and 16 at 30 s, at cold and warmed states: **1,536 darks/body/mode**. Record temperature if available, otherwise elapsed warm-up and ambient temperature. Hold out complete capture sequences. Split the 64 short darks into independent map-fit/check halves; retain a separate sequence to validate drift. Check existing darks for suitability before recapture. Flats from experiment 3 are needed to distinguish multiplicative PRNU from additive shading.

**Procedure / cost:** estimate black offset, stable pixel pattern and exposure/temperature-dependent dark current separately. Keep map coordinates tied to sensor origin, visible crop and CFA, and avoid double-subtracting metadata/optical-black correction. Start with cold/warm bracketing, not unconstrained interpolation. Compare no map, fit map and deliberately mismatched map on independent darks and real scenes. Frozen-r4 preprocessing pilot: **0 training G h**, allow 1–4 CPU h plus 1–2 G h inference. If useful, train matched corrected/uncorrected 24k arms (**8–28 G h**) and confirm at full budget above; changing preprocessing shifts r4's input distribution.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** own maps only, no copied calibration assets. E1/E2 plus E4 dark mean/row/column spectrum and E3 independent bright references. Report LOO against original and independently corrected references so preserving FPN is not rewarded. Stop if maps drift enough to add bias or remove real gradients; correction must fail closed on an unknown mode/temperature rather than silently apply a wrong body map.

### 3. Use measured shot gain plus sampled real dark residuals

**Hypothesis / why new:** round 6 fitted simplified distributions and interim K. Sampling dark residuals preserves code histograms, readout correlation and non-Gaussian structure without having to fit it. Combine SFRN/2025 sampling ideas [R3–4] with measured gain; do not globally widen every prior.

**Exact data:** experiment-2 library plus **two flats at each of 20 unsaturated illumination levels × eight ISOs = 320 flats/body/mode**, with one repeated sequence on another session. Fit variance from frame differences/2 to cancel stable scene/PRNU structure, by colour plane, ISO and shutter mode. Remove illumination drift; check linearity and conversion-gain discontinuities. Keep measured K in explicit DN/electron and normalised units with black/white ranges; do not substitute ISO or quantum efficiency directly for gain.

**Procedure / cost:** sample measured Poisson shot noise and add dark residuals after subtracting their independently fitted mean. Preserve row/column layout and crop phase; do not arbitrarily rotate structured dark patches. A sampled residual already contains read, row, column and quantisation effects: do not inject these twice. Test three matched arms: parametric measured K; sampled darks with measured K; sampled darks with bounded K jitter. Retain 25% broad-prior samples initially as a fixed, declared control choice. Three 24k arms **12–42 G h**, about 8–28 wall h on two GPUs; full confirmation only for the winner against the matched control. A cheap variant separately extends just the supported extreme-ISO range, preserving standard-ISO sampling probability.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** own flats/darks and clean pool; paper implementation only. E1 standard ISOs must survive alongside ISO 204800; E2 tails and 1/64 shadows are mandatory. Hold out dark sequences and real scenes independently. Check residual histograms and spatial spectra first, but stop unless real-burst/visual gains follow. Flatter simulator validation alone repeats round 6.

### 4. Learn only the noise components the empirical sampler misses

**Hypothesis / why new:** a compact PNNP/conditional-flow-style residual model might interpolate ISO/temperature and correlated tails better than scalar fits [R5–7]. It must outperform experiment 3, not merely Gaussian noise. Full dark-noise diffusion [R8] is a deferred comparator, not the first generator.

**Exact data:** library from 2–3, ideally **256 dark frames/ISO at four representative ISOs** spanning gain regimes, two thermal states, with a second session held out. Train on full temporal residuals or coordinate-aware patches; no noisy-image-minus-r4 residuals. Darks cannot identify signal-dependent shot noise, PRNU or signal crosstalk; use the flats and measured Poisson term for those questions.

**Procedure / cost:** remove deterministic shading, model row/column components explicitly, then initialise a small residual proxy/flow randomly with ISO, exposure and temperature conditioning. Dequantisation must match recorded code steps. Compare held-out likelihood where defined, tail probabilities and lag/row spectra, not just marginal histograms. Generator pilot **2–8 G h**, then two 24k denoiser arms (empirical versus learned) **8–28 G h**: **10–36 G h total**, roughly 6–22 wall h with staged dependencies. Full denoiser confirmation adds 68–268 G h; this estimate excludes a diffusion study. No runtime generator is needed if noise is cached for training.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** random proxy and denoiser, owner data only, no external generator/discriminator weights. Reimplement from papers; code licences listed previously are not verified permission to copy. E1/E2/E4 plus held-out dark *sessions*, not random neighbouring patches. Reject generators that memorise fixed maps or improve noise fit without real denoising. A per-pixel flow cannot by itself reproduce spatial covariance.

### 5. Few-shot camera/mode adaptation with real clean pairs

**Hypothesis / why new:** LED's lesson is to learn the remaining synthetic-to-real mapping from a few actual pairs [R10]. Round 7 used spatial neighbours, and round 5 added synthetic source images; neither supplied this signal.

**Exact data:** choose one target body/mode first, e.g. A5100 for new coverage. Capture **six training scenes**, two at each of three noise/digital-gain regimes, plus four independent development and ten acceptance scenes. Use experiment-1 independent native targets, 32 target frames plus four noisy inputs/setting. Six pairs is a lower-bound feasibility test, not adequate scene diversity. Log analog gain/ISO, exposure, digital lift and black levels separately; held-out intermediate settings test interpolation.

**Procedure / cost:** start from our own randomly trained generic base; freeze it for one arm and train randomly initialised small per-channel affine/adaptation branches, then compare a second arm adapting all weights, both with 50% synthetic replay. Any inherited weights are from that documented own-training lineage. Use 2k steps, evaluate, then at most 8k per arm; estimated **2–10 G h total**, 1–5 wall h on two GPUs once the generic base exists. Base training costs **17–67 G h/seed** and is not free. Linear branches can be folded where algebraically valid, following the published re-parameterisation idea, with numerical checks.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** owner paired data, own base, random adapters; do not copy the restricted implementation or use its checkpoint. E1/E2 for established cameras plus equivalent new-body pairs; leave an entire body out of generic training for a genuine transfer test. Stop on training-scene-only gains, adaptation of black offsets instead of noise, or regressions at unseen ISO/exposure. A per-body specialist is acceptable only with declared, tested scope.

### 6. Test noise normalisation and a variance-stabilised backbone

**Hypothesis / why new:** equalising Poisson-Gaussian statistics may be more useful than appending an inaccurate map [R11–12]. The AIM ablation gives contrary evidence [R9], so rank this below real-data corrections.

**Exact data:** measured per-channel K/read parameters from 3; owner binned pool and new native pairs from at least X1D II and A7 III. Reserve a third captured body entirely for transfer. Require negative/near-black flats, intermediate ISOs, conversion-gain boundaries and clipped ramps. Third-body absence limits the claim to within-camera generalisation.

**Procedure / cost:** compare three scratch arms on exactly the same noise draws: ordinary gamma-head NAFNet, affine noise normalisation, and generalised Anscombe/VST with a bias-aware inverse or calibrated expectation lookup. For `y = a·Poisson(x/a) + N(0,b)`, the usual GAT is `2/a × sqrt(max(a·y + 3a²/8 + b, 0))`; its truncation is many-to-one near black. Preserve original negative samples outside the transform and quantify mean bias rather than claiming reversible negative handling. Do not stack the existing gamma head blindly on VST output. Test oracle, 0.5×/2× and estimated parameters, including black-offset error. Three 24k arms **12–42 G h**, then winning full confirmation; transform calibration adds under 2 CPU h initially.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** independent implementation, own calibration and random bases; no YOND executable/weights or externally cleaned training pool. E1/E2 plus E3 colour bias near black and unseen-body real pairs. Evaluate correlated dark residuals too: scalar VST stabilises approximate variance, not FPN or covariance. Reject oracle-only benefits, clipping/black bias or extra inference passes that erase deployment value. A wrapper preserves four-channel I/O only after its calibrated parameters and inverse are made part of the deployment contract.

### 7. Retry self-supervision only with independent temporal samples

**Hypothesis / why new:** aligned repetitions remove the neighbour-pair signal mismatch. Noise2NoiseFlow supports this distinction [R17]; Blind2Sound suggests a separate single-image objective worth a small falsification test [R18], not another round-7 weight sweep.

**Exact data:** **20 new static scenes × 16 same-exposure repeats** at representative ISO 3200/25600 settings on the target body, with session splits (12 train/four dev/four preliminary test; final acceptance still separate). Darks diagnose shared bias/correlation. Scene brightness and focus must remain stable. No archive sibling from any evaluation day is eligible.

**Procedure / cost:** compare pure L2 temporal Noise2Noise on different frames, the existing spatial-neighbour loss, and synthetic-only control with matched camera shares, optimiser steps and compute. Use L2 for the mean-estimation argument; a nonlinear gamma/Charbonnier target objective has different expectation properties. Correct stable shading first and reject motion without denoising targets. Joint flow training is deferred until simple temporal N2N works. Three 24k arms **12–42 G h**; allow up to 2× forward-pass cost when mixing objectives. A Blind2Sound-inspired noise-aware blind-spot pilot gets a separate **4–14 G h** ceiling and requires an independently audited implementation, not a presumed drop-in loss.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** all random training lineages and own noisy measurements; no teacher pseudo-clean labels. E1/E2 and new-scene temporal evaluation; do not evaluate against a target containing the input. Distinguish zero-mean random noise from shared bias. Stop if temporal N2N cannot match synthetic control or residual structure survives; do not infer low-ISO suitability from high-ISO independence. This is less efficient than building means when enough clean repeats already exist, hence rank 7.

### 8. Preserve measured native texture with small deterministic losses

**Hypothesis / why new:** r4's binned targets suppress native spatial frequencies; a stronger perceptual prior cannot recover their missing measurements. With experiment-1 targets, a small gradient/multiscale penalty might improve true fine detail without the global smoothing/strength trade seen in round 5.

**Exact data:** at least **20 training and ten held-out native-target scenes** with fabric, bark, grass, fine text, low-contrast edges and point lights, each built from independent 32–64-frame means. Estimate reference uncertainty with disjoint half means; random textures and smooth fields form synthetic controls.

**Procedure / cost:** control is current loss; candidates add `0.02` or `0.05 ×` same-colour finite-difference L1 loss after explicitly normalising each term's scale on training data. Gate texture weights using an independent target's SNR, not noisy input gradients. Include low-pass reconstruction at two scales to constrain colour/large structure. These weights are starting ablations, not an established optimum. Three 24k arms **12–42 G h** with <20% estimated compute overhead. No learned perceptual encoder, adversarial loss or stochastic texture synthesis. Frequency losses are optional later; energy matching alone cannot establish phase-correct detail.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** owner native targets and deterministic filters, random bases. E1/E2/E3, especially texture MSE, line/edge profiles, residual scene structure and matched blind crops. Check clean/near-clean identity and pre-pass-on/off star survival. Reject ringing, false colour or plausible but reference-inconsistent detail, even if sharpness scores rise. Gradient losses can fit residual target noise; target uncertainty is a prerequisite.

### 9. Make saturation and highlight boundaries explicit

**Hypothesis / why new:** the documented cyan fringe is a clipped-sample/colour-prior failure that global noise broadening cannot solve. It is largely invisible to current LOO because values above 0.9 are excluded.

**Exact data:** **12 train/four dev/ten acceptance scenes** containing lamps, white speculars, coloured LEDs and bright edges against shadows. At base/3200/25600 capture four noisy inputs plus 32 independent same-exposure targets. Add exposure brackets only as diagnostic evidence for the location of saturation, not permission to reconstruct radiance above clipping. Synthetic channel-specific ramps must use actual white levels and all four CFA phases.

**Procedure / cost:** generate latent nonnegative photon counts, add measured electronics/black offset, then apply the sensor's observed quantisation/clipping sequence; do not clip negatives in the normalised pipeline. Compare existing augmentation with saturation-aware corruption/loss and a conservative per-photosite saturated-core passthrough, inspecting the transition band separately. Target is `min(noise-free signal, white)` in sensor units. Mask unknowable radiance errors rather than force hue recovery from saturated channels. Two 24k arms **8–28 G h**; a passthrough diagnostic needs no training. Check whether internal gamma/output transforms introduce the fringe before changing the loss.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** own captures and simulated physics, random models; no external HDR reconstruction targets. E1/E2 remain regression gates, while E3 needs **new** unclipped/near-clip/clipped masks, channel bias, fringe width and fixed-render hue comparisons against same-exposure means. Existing scripts require extension for these metrics; do not claim current LOO verifies highlights. Stop if highlight handling suppresses real small lights/stars or creates a visible passthrough seam.

### 10. Improve the measured quality/ANE-latency frontier

**Hypothesis / why new:** spending capacity at coarse scales, folding linear adaptation, or distilling our own improved teacher may preserve detail at lower latency. Architecture changes alone did not fix the data gap; perform this after 1–3 identify a better training signal.

**Exact data:** identical eligible manifests/noise draws from the winning data experiment, frozen E1/E2/E3, plus an export fixture bank of **200 synthetic 512-packed tiles** spanning near-black negatives, bright flats, impulses, structured tails, colour extremes and saturation. Include full-frame borders and two tile phases. Distillation teacher must be our own provenance-audited random-origin model and remain frozen.

**Procedure / cost:** first export random tiny graphs and measure conversion, fp16 range and actual device placement before training. Compare (a) NAFNet w32 four-stage with high-resolution blocks reduced and coarse blocks retained, (b) a reduced Restormer-style middle stage only, and (c) at most a tiny MambaIR/v2-inspired scan block at the coarsest scale. These are proposed local variants. Selective scans, gathers or custom kernels may fail export or run elsewhere; ANE suitability is **unverified**. No new hard/custom-kernel dependency is justified. Allocate **1–4 engineering days** for graph/latency probes; if unsupported, stop those arms. Three trainable 24k pilots **12–42 G h for NAF-like throughput**, reserve up to **24–84 G h** if other backbones run at half speed. Do not assume model parameters imply memory or latency.

Re-parameterisation is a separate exactness check: fold compatible parallel/affine convolution branches and compare before/after outputs; nonlinear gates/scans remain. Own-teacher distillation gets one random student arm with `L = L(clean, student) + 0.1 × L(student, own_teacher)` on eligible noisy/clean pairs, plus a student supervised-only control (**8–28 G h pilot**). Record teacher provenance and its outputs as algorithmic soft targets, never clean GT. It may compress a winner; it cannot validate or create independent detail. The supervised-only control is essential to detect a teacher-imposed texture ceiling.

**Clean-room / evaluation / stop:** random student/proxies, own teacher only, paper-based implementations and own measurements; recheck any copied code's exact licence first. E1/E2/E3 and full export/identity/border/seam checks. Proposed efficiency target: ≤30 ms/512 tile on the **same** device/settings as 38.9 ms baseline with ≤0.05 dB per-band loss and no visual regression; alternatively better real quality at the existing latency. Report end-to-end 50/100 MP time and memory. Stop a “lite” architecture that only saves parameters, needs unsupported operators or loses native texture. No ANE or GPU performance was measured in this sweep.

## Execution order and unresolved requirements

Start with one body and one shutter mode. Collect independent burst targets and dark/flat calibration together. Run 2's deterministic correction diagnostic while 1's target QA is prepared; then compare 1 and 3 against matched controls. Only after a reproducible real gain should 4–10 consume substantial training time. Do not run all ten full budgets speculatively. Normalisation, self-supervision and architecture have useful falsification pilots but weaker evidence than new measurements.

The primary blockers are captures, alignment/photometric QA, independent acceptance coverage and measured training throughput. Additional work is needed for corrected-reference comparisons, highlight masks, scene-level confidence intervals and some export probes. This task only adds the plan. No model is trained, promoted, exported or benchmark-submitted; no third-party repository state is changed.
