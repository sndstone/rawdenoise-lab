1. Two-Stage RAW Denoiser & 2× Super-Resolution — What It Does & How It Works
Bayer Mosaic → Bayer Mosaic · Clean-Room Weights · Shift-Equivariant LocalSCA

Instead of upscaling an already-demosaiced RGB image, this pipeline operates directly on signed linear Bayer RAW photosites (4 packed channels: R, G1, B, G2). First, the frozen main denoiser (r4_plain) removes shot and read noise while preserving linear highlight structure. Next, PhotoSRv2 reads both the defect-cleaned original RAW and the denoised guide to synthesize a true 2× wider & 2× taller Bayer mosaic (4× total photosites) that saves back as an exact uint16 .dng editable in any RAW converter.

Stage 1 · Main Denoiser r4_plain
23.82M · Frozen
  • Noise-aware gamma space: compresses signed linear RAW via x^0.4545 so shadow read noise and midtones share balanced dynamic range, predicts a residual, and inverts to linear.
  • 4-stage NAFNet U-Net: width 40, blocks [1,1,2,4] / 6 / [1,1,1,1], feathered 512×512 tiles (overlap 60) after correct_defects(k=8).
  • Highlight lock: protect_highlights(≥0.98) preserves original clipped photosite ratios via a 4-step feathered mask.
Stage 2 · PhotoSRv2 2× RAW SR
5.97M · Clean-Room
  • Dual-input compressive asinh: fuses asinh(y_pre) (fine unsoftened texture), asinh(d) (clean guide), and 4 per-CFA saturation flags so 200:1 speculars never ring.
  • Shift-equivariant LocalNAFNet + HR tail: reflect-padded LocalSCA(15,11,7,5) (zero tile seams) + 24-ch 2-layer 3×3 HR tail after PixelShuffle(2) atop CFA site_upsample(d).
  • Exact Bayer TTA + Floor Guard: averages a 2-fold spatial transpose (swapping G1↔G2, the unique exact 2× Bayer symmetry) with zero-blur shadow/white bounds.

Why Two Stages?
r4_plain handles heavy multi-ISO noise across 1× pixels; PhotoSRv2 focuses purely on sub-pixel 2× reconstruction while reading back crisp texture from y_pre.
Exact 2× Bayer Symmetry
At 2× scale, horizontal/vertical flips shift Bayer parity (R→G). Spatial transpose + G1↔G2 swap is the unique exact symmetry (+0.07 dB, zero color shift).
True RAW Deliverable
Outputs stay linear in native camera primaries, keeping full exposure latitude, white-balance freedom, and bit-exact uint16 DNG roundtrips.
